Native trees of Assam

Hollong (Dipterocarpus macrocarpus) is the State Tree of Assam. The Assam State Biodiversity Board calls it the tallest tree of Assam.
Nahar (Mesua ferrea) grows in the forests of southern Assam, along with other native trees.
Bamboo: 38 naturally growing species are recorded in Assam, and bamboo is important in people's daily life.

Tip: ask a local nursery or the Forest Department which native saplings suit your place. Sources are listed in the README.
